# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads define and oversee the testing strategy, manage quality assurance activities, and ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards across all delivery phases.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and test plans aligned with project scope and risk profile
- Create acceptance criteria frameworks and quality gates
- Manage manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and defects, prioritize fixes
- Validate that acceptance criteria are met before release
- Coordinate with developers and product leads on testability and edge cases

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and reliability
- Reduce post-release defects and customer impact
- Enable fast, confident releases through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and sprint ceremonies
- Defect triage and prioritization
- Test result summaries and quality reports
- Release readiness sign-off

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test automation, edge case identification, and testability design
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics, blockers, and release readiness status
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria alignment and user story testability
- **Technical Leads**: Review test plans for technical feasibility and architecture alignment

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define the technical direction, guide design decisions, review solutions for alignment with architecture, and identify technical risks and mitigation strategies.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate technical architecture and design patterns
- Review design proposals and code for technical soundness
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on technical standards and best practices
- Collaborate with DevOps on deployment and operational readiness

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and architectural rework
- Enable team learning and continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and technical guidance
- Risk identification and mitigation planning
- Cross-team technical coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, design reviews, and mentorship on best practices
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Collaborate on testability design and technical quality standards
- **DevOps Engineers**: Partner on deployment architecture and operational readiness requirements

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove team blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Maintain sprint board and tracking metrics
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration
- Shield the team from external distractions and scope creep

### Goals
- Enable consistent team velocity and predictability
- Improve team collaboration and psychological safety
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Reduce cycle time and unplanned work

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and standups
- One-on-one coaching and feedback
- Retrospective action item tracking
- Process improvement recommendations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on scheduling, escalations, and risk tracking
- **Developers**: Facilitate day-to-day collaboration and remove blockers
- **Product Managers**: Help clarify backlog items and acceptance criteria
- **All Roles**: Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback across the team

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment pipelines, cloud and on-premises infrastructure, monitoring, and operational readiness. They enable fast, reliable, and secure deployments.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, provisioning, and scaling
- Set up monitoring, logging, and observability
- Plan and execute deployments to staging and production
- Ensure security, compliance, and disaster recovery readiness
- Participate in incident response and post-mortems

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and secure deployments
- Maintain high uptime and system performance
- Reduce manual operational overhead

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and coordination
- Infrastructure design reviews
- On-call incident response
- Post-release monitoring and verification

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Support build pipeline setup, deployment practices, and environment management
- **Technical Leads**: Collaborate on infrastructure architecture and deployment strategy
- **Project Managers**: Provide deployment timelines, readiness status, and risk assessments
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Enable staging environment setup and smoke test infrastructure

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads own the user experience strategy, define design systems and patterns, and validate usability to ensure products are customer-centric and intuitive.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience strategy and design standards
- Create and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Design user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity mockups
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Ensure design accessibility and inclusive design practices
- Collaborate with developers on design implementation

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Maintain design consistency across products and platforms
- Reduce user friction and support load through better UX

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research and usability testing results
- Design system documentation and guidelines
- Design-to-development handoffs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on design implementation, component development, and design system maintenance
- **Product Managers**: Validate user research findings and design alignment with product strategy
- **Project Managers**: Provide design timeline estimates and dependency information
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Collaborate on usability test planning and user acceptance validation

---

## Stakeholder / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, prioritization input, and resource approval. They represent customer and business interests in project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment for initiatives
- Prioritize work based on business impact and resource constraints
- Approve project charter, budgets, and major scope changes
- Review progress and validate business outcomes
- Escalate organizational blockers and barriers
- Communicate project status to executive leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align project outcomes with organizational strategy
- Minimize business risk and compliance issues

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and gate decisions
- Monthly stakeholder status updates
- Business case and ROI reviews
- Executive briefings and announcements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide sponsorship, escalation support, and resource coordination
- **Product Managers**: Align on business outcomes, success metrics, and prioritization
- **Developers and Technical Leads**: Provide business context and trade-off decisions
- **All Roles**: Ensure organizational alignment and strategic context
